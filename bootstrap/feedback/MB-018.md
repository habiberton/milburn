- Severity: blocking
- Symptom: Tester replacement accepted an arbitrary nonempty candidate SHA instead of binding it to the validated handoff head.
- Root cause: Role policy checked only candidate-SHA presence and did not correlate it with the validated manifest.
- Resolution: Required a nonblank Tester candidate SHA exactly equal to the validated manifest and expectation head SHA.
